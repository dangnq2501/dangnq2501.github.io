A little background

From algorithms to systems.

I’m interested in how algorithmic ideas translate into efficient, practical machine learning systems. My current research at MBZUAI focuses on efficient machine learning systems and high-performance computing.

Previously, I worked on GPU-based compression and streaming algorithms at BioTuring, and optimized model training and inference on NVIDIA and AMD accelerators at MOREH. My research at AVITECH includes robust tensor decomposition, with two papers at ICASSP 2026.

Across research and engineering, my work spans GPU kernels, sparse-data compression, streaming algorithms, and federated learning. I enjoy connecting mathematical structure with the realities of memory, computation, and scale.
